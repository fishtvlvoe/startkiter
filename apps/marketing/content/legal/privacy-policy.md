title: Privacy Policy
---

StartKiter uses this site to explain the course, the lifetime GitHub code package, and the delivery flow.

The public site stores one necessary `consent` cookie for up to 30 days after you choose Allow or Decline. It records that choice so the consent banner does not keep appearing. The current public site does not load a third-party analytics provider.

If you submit the contact form, the information you enter is used to respond to your request. Do not include passwords, payment card details, or other sensitive information in that form. Account, order, and payment information is handled by the StartKiter application and its configured payment and delivery providers when you create an account or purchase the package.

For a privacy question, use the contact page and describe the request without sending secrets.
