Starting a site is easier when the path is visible. StartKiter breaks the work into lessons, practice, and checkpoints so you can move from an idea to a working result without guessing what comes next.

The package is built for people who want to make an AI service their own. You work through the product decisions, implement the important pieces, and validate the result as you go.

The goal is not to leave you with a static result you cannot touch. The course leads to a code package you receive through GitHub, ready for ongoing maintenance and deployment.

### What you finish

You finish with a clearer product shape, a working site, and code you can continue to own. The platform shell also leaves room to add modules when the next requirement arrives.
